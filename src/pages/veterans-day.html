How the Texas Roadhouse Veterans Day voucher works

  • Pick it up on Veterans Day. In 2026 vouchers are handed out on Wednesday, November 11, from 11 AM to 2 PM at every Texas Roadhouse — the same window as 2025.
  • Bring proof of service. A military ID, VA card or discharge papers (such as a DD-214).
  • Use it later. The 2026 voucher can be redeemed through May 31, 2027. (Last year's voucher worked for dine-in or to-go; ask your restaurant if you plan to order to-go.)
  • What it covers. In past years, a choice of about 10 entrées — for example a 6 oz sirloin — with two sides and a drink. Rolls come with every meal.

Some restaurants near large bases (for example Ft. Bliss, Ft. Campbell and Joint Base Lewis-McChord) have run the event differently in the past, so call your local Texas Roadhouse if you're near one.

Is the 2026 offer confirmed?

Yes. In late October 2026 Texas Roadhouse announced it is running the voucher event again this year: pickup on November 11 from 11 AM to 2 PM at every location, with vouchers good through May 31, 2027. Locations can still vary, so a quick call to your local restaurant is worth it.

Who qualifies

Veterans and active-duty service members with proof of service. In past years the voucher has been for the service member only — spouses and family members pay regular menu prices. If you're bringing family, the Early Dine menu (Mon–Thu afternoons at most locations) keeps their meals cheaper. Veterans Day [trh_year] is a Wednesday, so Early Dine applies at most locations that afternoon.

Tips for Veterans Day at Texas Roadhouse

  • Arrive near 11 AM — lines build quickly at lunch.
  • Pick up the voucher, then come back on a quieter weeknight to use it.
  • Ask your local restaurant whether it also offers a year-round military discount — this varies by location and isn't a chain-wide policy.

Other Veterans Day deals worth checking

Many casual-dining chains run Veterans Day offers (Applebee's, Chili's, Outback and others). Terms change every year, so check each chain's official announcement before you go.
