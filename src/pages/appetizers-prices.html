What each appetizer is

Cactus Blossom: price, calories and is it worth it?

The Cactus Blossom is Texas Roadhouse's signature appetizer. It costs about [trh_price id="cactus-blossom"] and has 2,250 calories before the sauce, more than most entrées. Split four ways, that's about $2.25 and about 560 calories each, which is good value for a group. For two people it's a lot of food on top of a meal with free rolls, so Fried Pickles or Rattlesnake Bites are better sizes for two.

Lowest-calorie and cheapest appetizers

  • Lightest: Grilled Shrimp (370 calories), Texas Red Chili cup (210–250), Fried Pickles (550) and Rattlesnake Bites (560).
  • Heaviest: Cactus Blossom (2,250), Tater Skins (1,320) and Cheese Fries (1,240).
  • Cheapest: Texas Red Chili cup, Deviled Eggs (at some locations), Grilled Shrimp and Fried Pickles.
