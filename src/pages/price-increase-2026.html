Quick answer: Texas Roadhouse raised menu prices by about 1.9% in early April 2026, and on its August 6, 2026 earnings call management said it planned about 1% more at the start of the fourth quarter. On a $25 steak dinner, 1% is roughly 25 cents — small per plate, but it adds up across the year.

Why Texas Roadhouse still feels cheaper than other steakhouses

The chain prices below inflation on purpose. Even with the 2026 increases, sales kept rising: comparable restaurant sales were up 6.2% in Q2 2026, with 3% more guest traffic. The company had 755 restaurants at the end of Q2 2026.

How to pay less after the increase

  • Early Dine (Mon–Thu afternoons at most locations).
  • Family Meals for 4–6 people.
  • Daily specials and the free VIP Club.
