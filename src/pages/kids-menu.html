Quick answer: The Texas Roadhouse kids menu is for children 12 and under. Kids meals cost about [trh_range category="kids"]. Most restaurants include a side and a drink. The cheapest choices are the hot dog and mac and cheese (about [trh_price id="all-beef-hot-dog"]), and the bigger Ranger Meals cost the most. Many restaurants also run a local kids eat free night, usually on Tuesday.

What comes with a kids meal

  • One side: for example steak fries, mashed potatoes, applesauce, corn, green beans or a baked potato. Loaded or premium sides may cost extra.
  • A drink: included at most restaurants. Check your receipt, because a few locations charge for drinks separately.
  • Rolls: free and refilled, the same as for adults.

Ranger Meals: the bigger kids meals

Ranger Meals are larger plates for kids with bigger appetites. Andy's Steak (a small sirloin) costs about [trh_price id="ranger-meal-andys-steak"], and the Chicken Critters Basket about [trh_price id="ranger-meal-chicken-critters-basket"]. Some restaurants also offer a small ribs basket. For a hungry 10–12-year-old, a Ranger Meal is still a few dollars cheaper than an adult 6 oz sirloin at [trh_price id="hand-cut-sirloin-6-oz"].

Does Texas Roadhouse have a kids eat free day?

Many restaurants do, but it is a local deal, not a national one. Each restaurant chooses whether to run it and on which night. The usual rules are:

  • Day: most often Tuesday. Some restaurants pick Monday, and many don't run it at all.
  • Who: kids 12 and under.
  • Catch: one free kids meal for each adult entrée you buy. A parent with three kids gets one free kids meal.

To find out whether your restaurant runs it, check its Facebook page or call. Local specials like this one and Wild West Wednesday are usually posted there, not on the national website.

What a family of four pays

Two adult 6 oz sirloins, two kids meals (Jr. Chicken Tenders and Mini-Cheeseburgers) and two adult sweet teas come to about [trh_sum ids="hand-cut-sirloin-6-oz,jr-chicken-tenders,mini-cheeseburgers,iced-tea-sweet-tea-20-oz" qty="2,1,1,2"] before tax and tip. To spend less:

  • Go on your restaurant's kids eat free night.
  • Eat during Early Dine (Monday–Thursday afternoons), when adult dinners cost about [trh_range category="early-dine"].
  • Get a Family Meal to go ([trh_range category="family-meals"], feeds about four).

FAQ

What age is the Texas Roadhouse kids menu for?

Kids 12 and under. Some restaurants let adults order from the kids menu, but it's up to the restaurant and isn't guaranteed.

Is there a kids birthday freebie?

The free VIP Club birthday reward is for the adult member. At many restaurants, staff will still do the birthday saddle and cheer for a child, so just tell your server.

Are kids meals cheaper during Early Dine?

No. Kids meals cost the same all day. Early Dine only discounts adult dinners.
