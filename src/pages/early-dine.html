Quick answer: Texas Roadhouse Early Dine is a discounted dinner menu served Monday through Thursday, usually 3–6 PM (some restaurants end at 5 PM). You get a full-size entrée with two sides and the free rolls for about [trh_range category="early-dine"], which is about $2.50–$5.50 less than the same dinner on the regular menu. Times and prices are set by each restaurant.

Texas Roadhouse Early Dine hours

Texas Roadhouse told The U.S. Sun in 2026 that Early Dine is available at its U.S. restaurants. However, each restaurant still sets its own cutoff time, so check your restaurant's page on the Texas Roadhouse website or call before you go. Plan to be seated before the cutoff time. Early Dine rules to know

  • Dine-in first. Early Dine is meant for dine-in. Some restaurants also honor it for to-go orders placed in the window, so ask when you call.
  • No reservations. Texas Roadhouse doesn't take reservations, but you can join the waitlist in the app or on the website before you leave home.
  • Drinks and appetizers are regular price. Only the Early Dine entrées are discounted. Alcohol is never discounted.
  • Kids still order from the kids menu. Kids meals are already about [trh_range category="kids"].

FAQ

What time is Early Dine at Texas Roadhouse?

At most restaurants Early Dine runs Monday through Thursday from opening (around 3 PM) until 6 PM. Some restaurants stop at 5 PM, so check your local restaurant.

Is Early Dine available on Friday?

Not at most restaurants. On Fridays and weekends, Texas Roadhouse opens earlier and runs lunch specials instead. A few restaurants do things differently, so call to check.

How many sides come with Early Dine?

Two, the same as the regular dinner. You can swap one side for a house or Caesar salad, and the rolls are free.

Is Early Dine the same as an early bird special?

Yes. Early Dine is Texas Roadhouse's name for its early bird menu: full-size dinners at a lower price for guests who eat in the late afternoon.

Why is Early Dine more expensive at my restaurant?

Each restaurant sets its own prices. Early Dine has gone up over the years, and some city and West Coast restaurants now charge $12.99–$14.99 for some dinners. The table above shows typical U.S. prices.
