Texas Roadhouse ribs prices and calories

Calories are Texas Roadhouse's own figures for the ribs only. Two sides and a roll usually add 400–800 calories. Prices vary by location by a dollar or two.

Half slab or full slab: which is the better deal?

A full slab has about twice as many ribs as a half slab, but it only costs about $6 more. If you're hungry, or you're happy to take leftovers home, get the full slab. If you want ribs plus something else, a half slab works well. Or skip both and get a combo, which pairs a half portion of ribs with a steak or chicken for a few dollars more than a half slab on its own.

FAQ

How much is a full slab of ribs at Texas Roadhouse?

About [trh_price id="full-slab-ribs"] at a typical U.S. restaurant, with two sides and rolls included. Some city and airport-area restaurants charge a few dollars more.

How many calories are in Texas Roadhouse ribs?

A half slab has 900 calories and a full slab 1,450, for the ribs only, according to Texas Roadhouse's nutrition menu. A Sidekick of Ribs added to another meal is 550 calories.

Can I get ribs during Early Dine?

At some restaurants. Ribs aren't on every Early Dine menu, so ask your server. A Sidekick of Ribs added to an Early Dine dinner is a cheap way to try them.
