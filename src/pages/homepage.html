What comes with your meal

  • Two sides with steaks, ribs, chicken, seafood and country dinners. Choices include baked or sweet potato, mashed potatoes, steak fries, buttered corn, green beans, seasoned rice, steamed vegetables, applesauce and a house or Caesar salad.
  • Unlimited fresh-baked rolls with honey cinnamon butter, free when you dine in.
  • Peanuts at the table at many locations.
  • Burgers and sandwiches come with steak fries instead of two sides.

Hand-cut steaks

Steak dinners come with two sides and rolls.

Fall-off-the-bone ribs

Texas Roadhouse ribs are pork ribs, slow-cooked and basted with BBQ sauce until the meat pulls off the bone. They come with two sides; a half slab is a full meal for most people, and a full slab is about [trh_price id="full-slab-ribs"].

Texas-size combos

Combos pair a steak or chicken with ribs or grilled shrimp. They cost a few dollars more than a single entrée but are the most food for the money if you want to try two things.

[trh_table category="combos"]

Dockside favorites (seafood)

The seafood side of the menu is short: grilled salmon, fried catfish and grilled shrimp, each with two sides. Grilled shrimp also appear as a "sidekick" add-on to any steak.

Chicken specialties

Chicken dinners are the best-value full meals after Early Dine. Chicken Critters are hand-breaded tenders; Smothered Chicken is topped with sautéed onions, mushrooms and jack cheese; Country Fried Chicken comes with cream gravy. All include two sides.

Country dinners

Country dinners are the comfort-food plates: pulled pork, grilled pork chops, country fried sirloin with gravy, beef tips and a vegetable plate built from your choice of sides.

Why Texas Roadhouse prices are different by location

Each restaurant sets its own prices to cover local wages, rent and beef costs, so the same plate can differ by a few dollars between states, and by more in big cities and airports. Texas Roadhouse also raises prices in small steps: about 1.9% in April 2026 and about 1% more at the start of the fourth quarter. See Texas Roadhouse price increase for the details.

How to order the right way at Texas Roadhouse

  • Use the app waitlist before you leave home; Friday and Saturday evenings get long waits.
  • Order steaks by size and doneness. Bigger cuts of the same steak cost more, so the menu price is the starting price.
  • Pick sides that cost extra on their own (loaded baked potato, house salad) to get more value from the two included sides.
  • Share a starter. The Cactus Blossom easily feeds three or four.
  • Feeding a group at home? A Family Meal usually beats ordering four separate dinners.
