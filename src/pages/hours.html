Quick answer: Most Texas Roadhouse restaurants open at 3 or 4 PM Monday–Thursday and around 11 AM Friday–Sunday. They usually close around 10 PM on weeknights and Sundays and 11 PM on Fridays and Saturdays. Texas Roadhouse closes only two days a year: Thanksgiving and Christmas Day.

Typical weekly hours

Hours are set by each restaurant. A few locations open for lunch on weekdays; many don't. Check your restaurant's page on the Texas Roadhouse website or app before you go.

Why Texas Roadhouse opens late on weekdays

Weekday lunch is slow for full-service steakhouses, so most locations open in the afternoon and start with the Early Dine menu — lower-priced dinners served on weekday afternoons at most locations.

FAQ

When does Texas Roadhouse open today?

On a weekday, most locations open at 3 or 4 PM. On Friday, Saturday and Sunday, most open around 11 AM.

Is Texas Roadhouse open on Thanksgiving?

No. It's closed on Thanksgiving Day — [trh_holiday id="thanksgiving"]. More here.

Does Texas Roadhouse serve lunch?

On weekends, yes — most restaurants open around 11 AM. On weekdays, only some locations open before 3 PM. See the lunch menu.
