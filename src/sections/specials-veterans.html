Military Discount and Veterans Day

Every Veterans Day, Texas Roadhouse hands out free meal vouchers to veterans and active-duty military. This year it falls on [trh_holiday id="veterans-day" format="l, F j"] ([trh_countdown id="veterans-day"]). Pickup times, the ID you need and whether this year's terms are confirmed are in our Texas Roadhouse Veterans Day guide.

There's no chain-wide everyday military discount. Some restaurants offer one, so ask your local Texas Roadhouse before you order.
