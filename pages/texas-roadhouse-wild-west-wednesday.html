Quick answer: Wild West Wednesday is a Texas Roadhouse Wednesday special: an 8 oz USDA Choice sirloin with two sides and the free rolls, for about [trh_price id="wild-west-wednesday-8-oz-sirloin"] depending on the restaurant. It is a local deal, not a national one. Many locations run it and some don't, so check with your restaurant before you go.

What you get on Wild West Wednesday

For comparison, a regular 6 oz hand-cut sirloin dinner is about [trh_price id="hand-cut-sirloin-6-oz"]. So at most participating restaurants you get a bigger steak for the same money or less.

Is Wild West Wednesday at every Texas Roadhouse?

No. Texas Roadhouse lets each restaurant set many of its own specials, and Wild West Wednesday is one of them. It isn't listed on the national Texas Roadhouse website. Food writers have found it at a wide range of prices: The Takeout reported $10.99 to $14.99 in April 2026, and Chowhound saw about $15, and as low as $11 at some stores, in 2025.

The quickest ways to check your restaurant:

  1. Look at its Facebook page. Most Texas Roadhouse restaurants have their own page and post their weekly specials there.
  2. Call and ask whether it runs Wild West Wednesday, the price, and whether it's all day or dinner only.
  3. Ask your server on any visit. Local specials are often on a table card rather than the printed menu.

Wild West Wednesday vs Early Dine

On a Wednesday afternoon, some restaurants run both. If you want the bigger steak, ask for Wild West Wednesday. If you'd rather have chicken or pulled pork, the Early Dine menu is cheaper.

Tips to get the most out of it

  • Go early. Wednesday evenings get busy at restaurants that run the deal, so join the waitlist in the Texas Roadhouse app.
  • Ask before you add. Add-ons such as grilled shrimp or sautéed mushrooms cost extra and are priced by each restaurant.
  • Drinks add up. Tea, lemonade or a soft drink is about [trh_price id="iced-tea-sweet-tea-20-oz"]; margaritas are several dollars more.

FAQ

What time does Wild West Wednesday start?

It depends on the restaurant. Some offer it from opening on Wednesday, and others only from dinner time, often around 4 or 5 PM. Call your restaurant to check.

Can I get Wild West Wednesday to go?

Usually not. Most participating restaurants offer it for dine-in only. For a cheap takeout dinner, look at Family Meals instead.

Does Wild West Wednesday include a drink?

No. The price covers the steak, two sides and rolls. Drinks are extra.
