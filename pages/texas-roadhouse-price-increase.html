Why Texas Roadhouse still feels cheaper than other steakhouses

The chain prices below inflation on purpose. Even with the 2026 increases, sales kept rising: comparable restaurant sales were up 4.3% in Q3 2026, with guest traffic up 0.7% and the average check up 3.6%. The company counted 840 restaurants across all its brands at the end of Q3.

What management said on November 5

  • Q4 price increase: about 1%, in place since the start of the quarter. The CFO said guests' ordering habits haven't meaningfully changed since then.
  • Sales: comparable sales rose 4.3% in Q3, but slowed sharply in September (+1.0%, after +5.9% in July and +6.8% in August). The first five weeks of Q4 were up 1.5%.
  • Costs in 2027: the company expects commodity costs up about 4% and wages and other labor up about 3%.
  • 2027 menu prices: no new increase was announced. The company's 2027 sales outlook only mentions the benefit of pricing already taken in 2026.

Costs are still rising, so another small increase in 2027 is possible, but that's our reading rather than a company statement. Management usually discusses pricing on its quarterly calls, and we'll update this page after the next one.

How to pay less after the increase

  • Early Dine (Mon–Thu afternoons at most locations).
  • Family Meals for 4–6 people.
  • Daily specials and the free VIP Club.
