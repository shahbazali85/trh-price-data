Texas Roadhouse deals that actually work

Why we don't list promo codes

Texas Roadhouse doesn't publish public promo codes for its online ordering, and we haven't found one we can confirm works. Coupon sites often show codes "verified 2 hours ago" with no way to check. Rather than send you to a checkout that won't accept them, we only list offers we can trace to Texas Roadhouse or a dated news report. If a real code appears, we'll add it here with the date we confirmed it.

How to get the most out of each deal

  1. Join the VIP Club at least a month before your birthday. Birthday offers are sent ahead of time, and new sign-ups may miss the current month.
  2. Buy gift cards during a bonus promotion, then use the bonus card on a later visit. Bonus cards usually have their own start and expiry dates.
  3. Eat early on weekdays. Early Dine prices beat the regular dinner menu for the same entrées.
  4. Feeding 4 or more? A Family Meal usually costs less than four separate dinners.
  5. Check today's specials on our Texas Roadhouse specials page before you go.
