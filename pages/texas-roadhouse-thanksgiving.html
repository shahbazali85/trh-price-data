FAQ

Is Texas Roadhouse open on Black Friday?

Yes, with regular hours at most locations.

Is Texas Roadhouse open on Christmas?

No — it's closed on Christmas Day. Many locations are open on Christmas Eve but may close early. Details: Texas Roadhouse Christmas hours.
