Want Texas Roadhouse for Thanksgiving? Pick it up the day before

Because restaurants are closed on the day, the practical move is a Family Meal picked up on Wednesday and reheated on Thursday. Family Meals typically cost [trh_range category="family-meals"] and feed about 4 people. Each comes with a family-size salad, two large sides and 12 rolls with honey cinnamon butter.

What it costs to feed 8 people

  • Two Family Meals — for example Pulled Pork ([trh_price id="family-sized-pulled-pork"]) plus Chicken Critters ([trh_price id="family-sized-chicken-critters"]). That gives you two mains, two salads, four sides and 24 rolls.
  • Drinks: a gallon of tea or lemonade is about [trh_price id="gallon-tea-or-lemonade"].
  • Extra rolls: about [trh_price id="extra-rolls"] per order, plus [trh_price id="extra-honey-cinnamon-butter"] for extra honey cinnamon butter.

That example comes to roughly $100 before tax for eight people. Steak packs cost more; pork chop or cheeseburger packs cost less.

When to order

  1. The weekend before: decide how many packs you need and check your restaurant's Wednesday hours on the Texas Roadhouse website or app.
  2. Monday or Tuesday: place the order online and choose a Wednesday pickup time. The Wednesday before Thanksgiving is one of the busiest to-go days of the year, so early slots go first.
  3. Wednesday: pick up, refrigerate within two hours, and keep salads and dressing separate.

How to reheat it on Thanksgiving

  • Meats and sides: covered with foil in a 325°F oven until hot all the way through. Food-safety guidance is to reheat leftovers to 165°F.
  • Steaks: a low oven (250°F) for 15–20 minutes keeps them from drying out better than a microwave.
  • Rolls: wrap in foil and warm for 5–8 minutes, then add the honey cinnamon butter.

For bigger groups

For 15 or more people, compare Family Meals with the Texas Roadhouse catering menu. Catering usually needs 2–3 days' notice, and the holiday week fills up.

FAQ

Is Texas Roadhouse open the day before Thanksgiving?

Yes. Most locations keep normal weekday hours, opening at 3 or 4 PM. Some open earlier for to-go orders, so check your restaurant.

Is Texas Roadhouse open on Black Friday?

Yes, with regular Friday hours at most locations. Black Friday is also when gift card bonus offers usually run; we add this year's terms once they're announced.

Is Texas Roadhouse open on Christmas?

No, it's closed on Christmas Day. Most locations are open on Christmas Eve but may close early. Details: Texas Roadhouse Christmas hours.
