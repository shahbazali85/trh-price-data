Christmas and New Year hours at a glance

Christmas Eve closing times are set by each restaurant. Check your location's page on the Texas Roadhouse website or app on the day. For every holiday this year, see the Texas Roadhouse holiday calendar.

Christmas Eve dinner without the wait

  • Go early. Restaurants that close early on Christmas Eve still get busy in the late afternoon. Arriving at opening time is the easiest way to get a table.
  • Use the waitlist. Join the waitlist in the Texas Roadhouse app before you leave home.
  • Or take it home. A Family Meal (typically [trh_range category="family-meals"], feeds about 4) picked up on the 23rd or early on the 24th covers Christmas Eve dinner. Two packs feed about 8.

Texas Roadhouse gift cards as Christmas gifts

Texas Roadhouse regularly runs "buy a gift card, get a bonus card" promotions.   • Bonus cards usually have their own valid-from and expiry dates, separate from the gift card itself.
  • Bonus cards often can't be used on the same visit they were earned, or on online orders. Read the terms on the card.
  • A gift card plus a note about Early Dine makes the card go further on weekday afternoons.

FAQ

Is Texas Roadhouse open on Christmas Day?

No. Christmas Day and Thanksgiving are the two days Texas Roadhouse closes.

What time does Texas Roadhouse close on Christmas Eve?

It varies by restaurant, and many close earlier than usual. Check your location's hours on the official website or app that day.

Is Texas Roadhouse open on New Year's Day?

Yes. Most locations open on New Year's Day; hours vary by location.
