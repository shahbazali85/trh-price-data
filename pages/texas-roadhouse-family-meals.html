What comes with a Family Meal

  • The main: the protein you choose — steaks, chicken, ribs, pulled pork, beef tips, pork chops or burgers.
  • A family-size salad: House or Caesar, with dressing.
  • Two large sides: usually picked from mashed potatoes, green beans, buttered corn, seasoned rice and steak fries.
  • 12 rolls with honey cinnamon butter.

Drinks aren't included. A half gallon of tea or lemonade adds about [trh_price id="half-gallon-tea-or-lemonade"] and a full gallon about [trh_price id="gallon-tea-or-lemonade"].

Is a Family Meal cheaper than ordering separately?

Usually, yes. Four Grilled BBQ Chicken dinners from the regular menu cost about 4 × [trh_price id="grilled-bbq-chicken"], while the BBQ Chicken Family Meal is about [trh_price id="family-sized-bbq-chicken"] — and it adds the large salad and extra rolls. The savings are smaller on steak packs, but you still skip buying four separate salads.

How to order

  1. Go to the Texas Roadhouse website or app and choose your restaurant — prices load for that location.
  2. Open the Family Packs section, pick your main, salad and two sides.
  3. Choose a pickup time. On busy days (Fridays, holidays, the day before Thanksgiving) order a few hours ahead.

Family Meals vs catering

For more than 8–10 people, two Family Meals often work out cheaper than catering. For bigger groups, compare with the Texas Roadhouse catering menu.

FAQ

How many people does a Texas Roadhouse Family Meal feed?

About 4 adults. Big eaters or a group of 5–6 usually add an extra side or a second protein.

Can I eat a Family Meal in the restaurant?

No — Family Meals are packed for takeout. In the dining room, order from the regular menu or the Early Dine menu.

Why is the price different at my Texas Roadhouse?

Each restaurant sets its own prices based on local costs. Restaurants in high-cost areas can be $5–$10 higher on the same pack.
