How the bonus card works

  • Buy: $50 in gift cards, physical or e-gift, on the Texas Roadhouse website during the promo window. $100 earns $20 in bonus cards, and so on up to the $100 bonus cap.
  • Receive: the bonus comes as a separate digital card sent to your email, not added to the card you bought.
  • Use: past bonus cards were good for food and drinks at U.S. restaurants. They have no cash value, and the exact expiry is printed on the card, so check it before you plan a visit.
  • Stack: you can pay one bill with several gift cards, including bonus cards.

Where to buy Texas Roadhouse gift cards

  • Texas Roadhouse website: physical cards (shipping is free and you can add a greeting card) or e-gift cards by email. Bonus offers have been online only.
  • In the restaurant: ask your server or at the host stand.
  • Grocery and retail stores: many supermarkets and pharmacies sell them on gift card racks. Store fuel or rewards points can be a small extra saving.
  • For businesses: Texas Roadhouse sells branded bulk cards and gives 10% off orders of $1,000 or more.

Texas Roadhouse also says it donates 10% of gift card sales to Homes For Our Troops, a charity that builds adapted homes for injured veterans.

How to check your Texas Roadhouse gift card balance

  1. Open the gift card page on texasroadhouse.com and find the balance check.
  2. Enter the card number (16–19 digits) and the PIN under the scratch-off strip on the back (4–8 digits).
  3. Or ask any Texas Roadhouse server or host to check it for you.

Treat a gift card like cash: Texas Roadhouse doesn't replace lost or stolen cards. Some states let you cash out a small leftover balance (for example under $15 in California or under $2.50 in Texas).

FAQ

Does Texas Roadhouse have a Black Friday gift card deal?

Texas Roadhouse runs gift card specials around the holidays, and in 2025 it ran a one-day $10-per-$50 bonus on November 5, a Cyber Monday bonus around November 30 – December 1, and another one-day offer on December 26. It hasn't announced this season's dates yet. The deal box at the top of this page changes to "confirmed" when it does.

Do Texas Roadhouse gift cards expire?

Regular gift cards you pay for don't expire. Promotional bonus cards are different, so read the date printed on the bonus card.

Can I use a Texas Roadhouse gift card for to-go orders?

Yes. Gift cards work for dine-in and to-go at U.S. Texas Roadhouse restaurants, including when you order online for pickup.
