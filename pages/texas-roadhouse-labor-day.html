Quick answer: Yes, Texas Roadhouse restaurants are open on Labor Day. The next Labor Day is [trh_holiday id="labor-day"]. On a normal Monday most locations open at 3 or 4 PM, but holiday hours can differ, so check your restaurant's hours on the Texas Roadhouse website or app that morning.

Labor Day hours at Texas Roadhouse

  • Open: all regular locations stay open on Labor Day. Texas Roadhouse closes only on Thanksgiving and Christmas Day.
  • Opening time: set by each restaurant. Some open earlier than a usual Monday for the holiday crowd; others keep weekday hours.
  • Closing time: usually the normal weeknight close, around 10 PM.

All the other holidays are on our Texas Roadhouse hours and holiday calendar.

Are there Labor Day specials?

Texas Roadhouse doesn't usually run a national Labor Day deal. The regular ways to save still apply:

  • Early Dine — lower-priced dinners on weekday afternoons at most locations. Ask whether your restaurant runs it on the holiday Monday.
  • Family Meals — typically [trh_range category="family-meals"], feed about 4. Good for a cookout or a long weekend at home.
  • Daily specials and the free VIP Club.

Tips for a busy holiday

  • Join the waitlist in the Texas Roadhouse app before you leave home.
  • Late afternoon fills up fast on holiday Mondays; arriving right at opening is the easiest way to get a table.
  • For a group, order to-go ahead of time and pick a pickup time.
