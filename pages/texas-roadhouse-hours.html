Most Texas Roadhouse restaurants open at 3 or 4 PM Monday–Thursday and around 11 AM Friday–Sunday. They usually close around 10 PM on weeknights and Sundays and 11 PM on Fridays and Saturdays. Texas Roadhouse closes only two days a year: Thanksgiving and Christmas Day.

Is Texas Roadhouse closed on Thanksgiving and Christmas?

Yes. Thanksgiving and Christmas Day are the only two days a year that Texas Roadhouse restaurants close, so staff can spend the holiday at home. This year that means:

  • Thanksgiving, [trh_holiday id="thanksgiving" format="l, F j"]: closed all day. Restaurants are open the Wednesday before (usually from 3 or 4 PM) and on Black Friday, [trh_holiday id="black-friday" format="F j"], from about 11 AM.
  • Christmas Eve, [trh_holiday id="christmas-eve" format="l, F j"]: open at most restaurants, but many close early, so check your location's hours that morning.
  • Christmas Day, [trh_holiday id="christmas-day" format="l, F j"]: closed all day.
  • New Year's Eve and New Year's Day: open at most restaurants.

Texas Roadhouse for Thanksgiving or Christmas at home

Since the restaurants are closed on both days, the usual workaround is a Family Meal picked up the day before. A Family Meal costs about [trh_range category="family-meals"] and comes with a family-size salad, two large sides and 12 rolls, enough for about four people. Two packs, say Pulled Pork ([trh_price id="family-sized-pulled-pork"]) and Chicken Critters ([trh_price id="family-sized-chicken-critters"]), plus a gallon of tea ([trh_price id="gallon-tea-or-lemonade"]) feed eight for roughly $105 before tax.

  • Order early in the week. The day before Thanksgiving is one of the busiest to-go days of the year, and the good pickup times go first. Order online on Monday or Tuesday.
  • Reheat gently. Cover meats and sides with foil and heat at 325°F until hot through (165°F inside). Steaks do better in a 250°F oven for 15–20 minutes than in a microwave. Wrap the rolls in foil for 5–8 minutes.
  • Feeding 15 or more? Compare Family Meals with the catering menu. Catering usually needs 2–3 days' notice, and holiday weeks book up.
  • Christmas Eve dine-in: arrive near opening time or join the waitlist in the app before you leave, since restaurants that close early still fill up in the late afternoon.

Typical weekly hours

Hours are set by each restaurant. A few locations open for lunch on weekdays; many don't. Check your restaurant's page on the Texas Roadhouse website or app before you go.

Why Texas Roadhouse opens late on weekdays

Weekday lunch is slow for full-service steakhouses, so most locations open in the afternoon and start with the Early Dine menu — lower-priced dinners served on weekday afternoons at most locations.

FAQ

When does Texas Roadhouse open today?

On a weekday, most locations open at 3 or 4 PM. On Friday, Saturday and Sunday, most open around 11 AM.

Is Texas Roadhouse open on Thanksgiving?

No. It's closed on Thanksgiving Day — [trh_holiday id="thanksgiving"].

Is Texas Roadhouse open on Christmas Eve?

Yes, at most locations, but many close early. Closing times are set by each restaurant, so check yours on the day.

Is Texas Roadhouse open on Black Friday?

Yes. Most restaurants open around 11 AM on Black Friday, the same as a normal Friday.

Does Texas Roadhouse serve lunch?

On weekends, yes — most restaurants open around 11 AM. On weekdays, only some locations open before 3 PM. See the lunch menu.
