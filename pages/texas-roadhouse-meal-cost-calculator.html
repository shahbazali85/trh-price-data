How to spend less at Texas Roadhouse

  • Go early on a weekday. The Early Dine menu knocks a few dollars off popular dinners on weekday afternoons at most locations.
  • Feeding four or more? A Family Meal ([trh_range category="family-meals"]) is usually cheaper than four separate entrées.
  • Fill up on the free stuff. Rolls with cinnamon butter are free and refilled, so you can skip the appetizer.
  • Drink water or a fountain drink. Margaritas and beer can add $5–$10 per person.
  • Buy discounted gift cards. During gift card promotions you get a $10 bonus card for every $50.

How much should I tip at Texas Roadhouse?

For table service in the U.S., 15–20% of the bill before tax is standard. To-go orders don't require a tip, though many people leave a few dollars for large orders.
