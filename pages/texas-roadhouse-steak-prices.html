Lowest-calorie steak meals

The 6 oz sirloin (250 calories) and 6 oz Dallas Filet (270) are the lightest cuts. Sides make the biggest difference: green beans are about 100 calories, a house salad about 230 and a plain baked potato about 380, while steak fries and seasoned rice are about 360 each. A 6 oz sirloin with green beans and a house salad comes to about 580 calories before rolls and dressing.

FAQ

What is the cheapest steak at Texas Roadhouse?

The 6 oz USDA Choice sirloin at about [trh_price id="hand-cut-sirloin-6-oz"], or about [trh_price id="early-dine-6-oz-sirloin"] during Early Dine. The Road Kill chopped steak (about [trh_price id="road-kill"]) is cheaper but isn't a hand-cut steak.

How many calories are in a Texas Roadhouse 6 oz sirloin?

250 calories for the steak alone, according to Texas Roadhouse's nutrition menu. Two sides and a roll usually add another 400–800 calories.

What is the best steak at Texas Roadhouse?

For flavor and juiciness, the Ft. Worth Ribeye. It is also the best value per ounce. For tenderness, the Dallas Filet. For a lean, affordable everyday steak, the sirloin.

Is prime rib available every day?

Not everywhere. Many restaurants serve prime rib mainly on weekends, and it can sell out, so call ahead if you're going just for it.
